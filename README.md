# hanzi-studio
Chinese handwriting practice

Vocabulary lists: HSK 2025 syllabus data sourced from chinesetest.cn via https://github.com/chelsea6502/hsk-2025-data. Russian translations reuse the project's existing dictionary entries; concise glosses were added for new words.

HSK 2.0 additions: 189 common words missing from the HSK 2025 list, selected from https://github.com/LiudmilaLV/json_hsk and integrated into levels 1–6.

HSK 3.0 (2021) additions: 513 unique words missing from the HSK 2025 list, added at their source levels 2–6. Vocabulary and pinyin are from HSK University (https://github.com/ayman-ezz/hsk-3.0-word-list, CC BY 4.0); Russian glosses are from Zitou (https://zitou.app, CC BY 4.0). The 2021 standard uses bands 1–6 and a combined 7–9 band, so its level boundaries can differ from the 2025 exam syllabus.
